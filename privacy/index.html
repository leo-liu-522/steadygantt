Privacy Policy

SteadyGantt for Jira · Effective August 13, 2026

Summary: SteadyGantt has no servers of its own. Your Jira data never leaves Atlassian's infrastructure, nothing is sent to us or to any third party, and the app contains no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts.

Who we are

SteadyGantt is a Jira Cloud app built on the Atlassian Forge platform, developed and operated by an independent developer. Contact: steadygantt@outlook.com.

What data the app accesses

When you open SteadyGantt inside a Jira project, it reads — through Atlassian's official APIs and only for the duration of your session — the following fields of that project's issues: summary, issue type, parent/epic relationship, start date, due date, status, assignee display name, and issue links. This is the minimum needed to draw the chart.

The app writes to Jira in exactly one case: after you review a change summary ("diff") and explicitly confirm it. Only the date fields you checked are written. Every sync can be undone in one click.

What data the app stores

  • View preferences (zoom level, scroll position, collapsed groups, panel width) are saved in Forge storage — a storage service hosted entirely by Atlassian inside your Jira site's data boundary — keyed by your Atlassian account ID and the project key, so your view is restored on any device.
  • A local cache of the task list is kept in your own browser's localStorage so the chart reopens instantly. This cache never leaves your browser and is refreshed automatically.

Nothing is stored on our own systems, because we do not operate any. We cannot see your Jira data, your view preferences, or anything else about your site.

Data leaving Atlassian

None. The app declares no external endpoints and the Forge platform enforces this at runtime — it is technically incapable of sending your data outside Atlassian. The production version of SteadyGantt is eligible for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program, which certifies exactly this property.

Analytics, cookies, and trackers

None. The app sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, and includes no analytics or telemetry of any kind.

Data retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage in accordance with Atlassian's platform data lifecycle.
  • The browser cache can be cleared at any time through your browser settings, and is overwritten on each visit.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, a future feature that requires new permissions), this page will be updated before the change ships, and the change will be visible in the app's requested scopes, which Atlassian requires administrators to re-approve.

Contact

Questions about privacy are answered within 24 hours: steadygantt@outlook.com.
