How to actually read this table

Don't trust the average rating alone. The Marketplace switched its review scale in 2026, and averages smooth over years of history. Open the newest ten reviews of any app you shortlist — an app can hold a decent average while its recent reviews tell a different story.

Installs measure history, not quality. The biggest apps here have been listed for a decade. Look at installs together with the rating, and check "last updated" for signs of active maintenance.

Trial any of these safely

Gantt apps write to your Jira. Some reviewers learned that the hard way — reset dates, mass-touched "Updated" fields, even deleted issues. Three tests to run in the first ten minutes of any trial:

  1. Install and open the app, change nothing. Then check whether the "Updated" field on your issues moved.
  2. Remove a project or issue from the chart view. Then search Jira to confirm the issue still exists.
  3. Drag a bar, close the tab without saving. See what got written to Jira, and whether your draft survived.

Run them on a test project first. Every incident above comes from a public Marketplace review — see the per-app breakdowns for BigGantt and WBS Gantt-Chart. The full 10-test version, printable: the Jira Gantt data-safety checklist.
